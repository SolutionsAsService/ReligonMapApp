# R1 — Critic: Rupture (Shklovsky)

**Anchor used:** *Art as Technique* (Shklovsky): art exists "to make the stone stony"; it roughens form to slow perception and defeat the habit of recognition. Smoothness is perception going automatic.

**Observed:**
- home-desk.png: a cream Equal Earth map, a Didone headline, pill chips, a soft beige ribbon. It is the newspaper explainer we have seen a thousand times. You recognise it before you read it.
- timeline-top-desk.png: the folded axis with zigzag seams, "fold · years 2.4× wider →" and "3.8×". Evidence: the ratios are printed, ×9.0 last vs first.
- families_africa_html-desk.png: Yoruba, Vodun, Candomblé and Santería are joined by dashed "born from" arcs across the Atlantic. In the lineage chart, "Yoruba religion (Ifá, Orisha)" is printed over "fold · years 3.8× wider".

- timeline-top-desk.png axis: "1000 BCE" and "500 BCE" run together ("1000 BC500 BCE").

**Findings:**
- F1 [claim] The fold is the one real estrangement. The seam makes the eye stop and admit that time is drawn unequally. It is the only place the form resists me.
- F2 [defect] The fold is then softened everywhere else. On the home ribbon it is a faint dotted tick, so the rupture is explained away in a caption.
- F3 [defect] The landing is all smoothness: tasteful palette, rounded chips, a dot map of the familiar kind. Recognition wins at first sight.
- F4 [claim] The heavy end cap and the written causes on Lost roughen the line. An ending reads as a blow, not a fade.
- F5 [defect] Collisions on the fold (Africa lineage label; "1000 BC500 BCE") are accidental roughness. That is noise, not device.
- F6 [premise] Panel blind spot: I do not judge legibility for an audience. The 26/26 PASS measures that, not strangeness.

**Verdict:** OBJECT
