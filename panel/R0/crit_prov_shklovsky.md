# Critic: Provocateur (Shklovsky), R0

- **Anchor used:** Shklovsky, *Art as Technique*. Habit devours things, and art exists to make the stone *stony* by slowing perception down. Recognising an object by its outline is the enemy.

- **Observed:** old-full.png is a stock d3 bar chart. It uses two default colours (#69b3a2, #404080) and an axis from -2000 to 2025. Every living bar stops at a hard-coded 2025 wall (religions.json). The title promises "Relationships", but script.js draws none. dead-religons.json (12 extinct faiths, each with a `reason_for_dying_out`) is never fetched (evidence.md).

- **Findings:**
  - F1 [defect] The form is a habitual Gantt chart. The eye recognises "chart" and stops, so faith becomes a project schedule.
  - F2 [defect] The strangest material, the dead faiths and their causes of death, is buried in an unread file.
  - F3 [defect] The shared 2025 edge makes every bar the same rectangle, which is habit drawn as data.
  - F4 [defect] Christianity's 14 children spill into the Islam and Judaism rows. This is an accident, not a device, and an accident does not estrange.
  - F5 [claim] "Relationships" exists only in the title. There is no geography and no lineage.
  - F6 [premise] The brief's "lite earth" risks the new habit: a spinning globe with dots.

- **Verdict:** OBJECT

- **Requested change:**
  - F1: Choose one device that makes time felt (strata or rivers) instead of bars.
  - F2: Lead with the dead and print the cause of death as text.
  - F3: Leave living faiths open-ended, so that only the dead have endings.
  - F4: Make overlaps deliberate or remove them.
  - F5: Draw origin and lineage as real links.
  - F6: Make any globe strange, not a default orb.
