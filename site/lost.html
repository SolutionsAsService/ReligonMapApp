Lost religions

19 of the 91 traditions here are no longer practised as living religions. They are listed in the order they ended. Most did not simply die: they were banned, conquered, absorbed, or became something else. One has a modern revival listed here as its own tradition: Heathenry, from Germanic & Norse religion.

  1. Sumerian religion c. 3500 BCE – c. 2000 BCE

    Ended c. 2000 BCE. Absorbed into Akkadian and Babylonian religion.

  2. Historical Vedic religion c. 1500 BCE – c. 500 BCE

    Ended c. 500 BCE. Grew into classical Hinduism, Buddhism and Jainism reacted against it.

  3. Babylonian & Assyrian religion c. 2000 BCE – c. 100 CE

    Ended c. 100 CE. Faded under Persian, Hellenistic and Parthian rule.

  4. Ancient Roman religion c. 753 BCE – 392 CE

    Ended 392 CE. Banned by Theodosius I in 391–392.

  5. Canaanite & Phoenician religion c. 2000 BCE – c. 400 CE

    Ended c. 400 CE. Roman conquest and Christianisation.

  6. Mithraism c. 80 CE – c. 400 CE

    Ended c. 400 CE. Banned with the other pagan cults by 392.

  7. Ancient Greek religion c. 1400 BCE – 529 CE

    Ended 529 CE. Christian emperors banned the cults (391–392); philosophy schools closed in 529.

  8. Ancient Egyptian religion c. 3100 BCE – c. 537 CE

    Ended c. 537 CE. Christianisation; Justinian closed the last temple, at Philae.

  9. Celtic religion c. 600 BCE – c. 600 CE

    Ended c. 600 CE. Roman conquest and Christianisation.

  10. Germanic & Norse religion c. 500 BCE – c. 1100

    Ended c. 1100. Christianisation of the Germanic peoples and of Scandinavia.

  11. Slavic religion c. 500 CE – 1168

    Ended 1168. Christianisation; Arkona fell to the Danes in 1168.

  12. Baltic religion c. 500 CE – 1387

    Ended 1387. Crusades and the baptism of Lithuania in 1387.

  13. Ājīvika c. 500 BCE – c. 1400

    Ended c. 1400. Faded away in medieval South India; no texts of its own survive.

  14. Mexica (Aztec) religion 1325 – 1521

    Ended 1521. Spanish conquest, 1521.

  15. Inca religion c. 1200 – 1572

    Ended 1572. Spanish conquest, 1572.

  16. Manichaeism c. 240 CE – c. 1600

    Ended c. 1600. Persecuted in both the Roman and Chinese empires; it outlived its rivals in China and vanished there last.

  17. Maya religion c. 1000 BCE – 1697

    Ended 1697. Spanish conquest; the last kingdom fell in 1697.

  18. Bábism c. 1844 – c. 1866

    Ended c. 1866. Most followers became Bahá'ís in the 1860s.

  19. State Shinto 1868 – 1945

    Ended 1945. Abolished by the Allied Shinto Directive, 15 December 1945.

91 traditions, 8 families, 3500 BCE to 2026. Dates marked c. are approximate; see Sources & method.
