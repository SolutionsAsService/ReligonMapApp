The line counts how many of the 91 traditions listed here were alive in each year. The axis is folded: a year after 1500 is drawn 9.0× wider than one before 500 BCE; the thin line above it shows the same years at true scale.

Gridlines are drawn every 100 years in all three stretches, so their spacing is the scale: 9.5, 22.6, 86.0 chart units apart before 500 BCE, from 500 BCE to 1500, and after 1500. Line lengths compare honestly only inside one stretch; each row's title and the table give its length in years.
