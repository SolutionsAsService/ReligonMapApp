Germanic & Norse religion

The gods Odin, Thor and Freyja, the world-tree and the sagas.

Dates
c. 500 BCE – 1100
Origin
Scandinavia and northern Germany (region)
Branches
Heathenry (Ásatrú)

About the date: Iron Age roots; Norse forms are best known from the Viking Age. Sweden is the last kingdom to become Christian, around 1100.

How it ended (1100): Christianisation of the Germanic peoples and of Scandinavia.
