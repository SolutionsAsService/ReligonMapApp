Sumerian religion

The oldest recorded religion, of city gods such as Inanna and Enlil housed in ziggurats.

Dates
c. 3500 BCE – c. 2000 BCE (c. 1,500 years)
Origin
Uruk
Branches
Babylonian & Assyrian religion

About the date: Temples at Uruk from the 4th millennium BCE. Sumerian ends as a spoken language around 2000 BCE; its gods live on in Babylonian religion.

How it ended (2000 BCE): Absorbed into Akkadian and Babylonian religion.
