The cults of Marduk, Ashur and Ishtar, and the astronomy that served them.

Dates
c. 2000 BCE – c. 100 CE (c. 2,099 years)
Origin

About the date: The last datable cuneiform temple tablet is from about 75 CE. Some cults survived later at Harran.

How it ended (100 CE): Faded under Persian, Hellenistic and Parthian rule.
