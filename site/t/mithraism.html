Mithraism

A Roman mystery cult of the god Mithras, popular with soldiers, worshipped in underground temples.

Dates
c. 80 CE – c. 400 CE (c. 320 years)
Origin
Rome (region)

About the date: First attested in the late 1st c. CE; gone by about 400. (The old file stored this as '1st century', which broke its JSON.)

How it ended (400 CE): Banned with the other pagan cults by 392.
