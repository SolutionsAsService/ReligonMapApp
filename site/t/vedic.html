Indian (Dharmic)

Historical Vedic religion

The fire-sacrifice religion of the Vedas, the oldest layer beneath Hinduism.

Dates
c. 1500 BCE – 500 BCE
Origin
Punjab, Sapta Sindhu (region)
Branches
Hinduism

About the date: The Rigveda is usually dated c. 1500–1200 BCE. Its sacrificial religion turns into classical Hinduism from about 500 BCE; it did not die so much as become something else.

How it ended (500 BCE): Grew into classical Hinduism, Buddhism and Jainism reacted against it.
