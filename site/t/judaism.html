Abrahamic

Judaism

The covenant religion of the people of Israel, centred on the Torah. After the Temple's destruction in 70 CE it continued as rabbinic Judaism, the root of every Jewish movement today.

Dates
c. 1200 BCE – present
Origin
Judean and Samarian hill country (region)
Followers
14.8 million Pew Research Center, 2020 estimates
Branches
Samaritanism, Christianity, Karaite Judaism, Hasidic Judaism, Reform Judaism, Orthodox Judaism, Conservative Judaism

About the date: Israelite religion is attested in the Iron Age hill country from about 1200 BCE; worship of one God alone takes its lasting shape around the Babylonian exile (6th c. BCE). Tradition dates Abraham earlier.
