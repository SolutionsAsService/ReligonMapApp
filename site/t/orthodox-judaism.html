Orthodox Judaism

The movements that hold the written and oral law as binding and unchanged, organised as such in reply to the Jewish Enlightenment and Reform.

Dates
c. 1820 – present
Origin
Central Europe (Hungary, Germany) (region)

About the date: Rabbinic practice is ancient; 'Orthodox' as a self-conscious movement forms in early-19th-c. Central Europe, in answer to Reform.

No spread route is recorded for Orthodox Judaism.
