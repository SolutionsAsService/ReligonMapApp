Chinese folk religion

The worship of ancestors, local gods and spirits that underlies Chinese life, mixing freely with Daoism and Buddhism.

Dates
c. 1250 BCE – present (c. 3,275 years)
Origin
Anyang
Branches
Confucianism, Daoism

About the date: Shang oracle bones from c. 1250 BCE are the earliest written record of the ancestor and spirit cults that run through Chinese religion.
