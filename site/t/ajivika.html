Indian (Dharmic)

Ājīvika

An ascetic school that held that everything is fixed by destiny (niyati).

Dates
c. 500 BCE – c. 1400 (c. 1,899 years)
Origin
Shravasti (region)

About the date: Founded by Makkhali Gosala, a contemporary of the Buddha and Mahavira. Last mentioned in Tamil inscriptions of the 14th c.

How it ended (1400): Faded away in medieval South India; no texts of its own survive.
