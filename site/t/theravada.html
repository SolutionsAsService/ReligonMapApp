Indian (Dharmic) · Buddhism

Theravada

The 'way of the elders', with the Pali canon; the Buddhism of Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand, Laos and Cambodia.

Dates
c. 250 BCE – present (c. 2,275 years)
Origin
Anuradhapura, Sri Lanka

About the date: Its lineage reaches Sri Lanka with Mahinda in the 3rd c. BCE; the name Theravada is applied later.
