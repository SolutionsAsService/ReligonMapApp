Karaite Judaism

A Jewish movement that accepts the written Hebrew Bible alone and rejects the authority of the rabbinic oral law.

Dates
c. 760 CE – present
Origin
Baghdad

About the date: Traced to Anan ben David in Baghdad around 760; the name Karaite is used from the 9th c.
