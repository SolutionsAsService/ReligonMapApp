Ancient Roman religion

The state cult of Jupiter, the household gods and the deified emperors.

Dates
c. 753 BCE – 392 CE
Origin
Rome

About the date: From Rome's traditional founding (753 BCE) to the Theodosian ban on pagan sacrifice (391–392). The old file's 380 Edict of Thessalonica made Christianity official but did not ban the old cults.

How it ended (392 CE): Banned by Theodosius I in 391–392.
