Lingayatism

A Shaiva movement that rejected caste and temple ritual; its members wear a personal linga.

Dates
c. 1160 – present
Origin
Basavakalyan, Karnataka

About the date: Grows around Basava at Kalyana in the 12th c.

No spread route is recorded for Lingayatism.
