Iranian

Manichaeism

Mani's religion of light against darkness. For a millennium it reached from Carthage to China.

Dates
c. 240 CE – c. 1600 (c. 1,360 years)
Origin
Ctesiphon

About the date: Mani began preaching in 240. Last traces are in Fujian, China, around the 16th–17th c.

How it ended (1600): Persecuted in both the Roman and Chinese empires; it outlived its rivals in China and vanished there last.
