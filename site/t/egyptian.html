Ancient Egyptian religion

The gods of the Nile, the pharaoh's divine kingship and the judgement of the dead.

Dates
c. 3100 BCE – c. 537 CE (c. 3,636 years)
Origin
Abydos and the Nile valley (region)

About the date: From the unification of Egypt, c. 3100 BCE, to the closing of the Isis temple at Philae, dated c. 535–537 CE.

How it ended (537 CE): Christianisation; Justinian closed the last temple, at Philae.
