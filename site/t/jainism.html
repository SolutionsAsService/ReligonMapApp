Jainism

A path of non-violence toward every living being and self-discipline toward the soul's release.

Dates
c. 500 BCE – present (c. 2,525 years)
Origin
Vaishali, Bihar (region)
Followers
4.45 million in India (2011) Census of India 2011 (India only)

About the date: Mahavira is placed in the 6th–5th c. BCE; Jains hold him to be the last of 24 teachers, the 23rd of whom, Parshvanatha, is earlier.
