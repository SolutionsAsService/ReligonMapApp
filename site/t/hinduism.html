Indian (Dharmic) · Historical Vedic religion

Hinduism

A family of traditions sharing the Vedas, dharma, karma and rebirth, and devotion to many forms of the divine. The third largest religion.

Dates
c. 500 BCE – present (c. 2,525 years)
Origin
Ganges plain (region)
Followers
1,200 million worldwide (2020) Pew Research Center, 2020 estimates
Branches
Vaishnavism, Shaivism, Shaktism, Smartism

About the date: Classical Hinduism takes shape from about 500 BCE as Vedic ritual meets the epics, the Upanishads and local devotion. Many Hindus see their dharma as without beginning.
