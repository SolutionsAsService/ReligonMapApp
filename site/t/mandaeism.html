Mandaeism

A small gnostic baptismal religion that honours John the Baptist, long rooted in the marshes of southern Iraq and Iran.

Dates
c. 100 CE – present
Origin
Southern Mesopotamia (region)

About the date: Origins between the 1st and 3rd c. CE are debated; Mandaean texts point to the Jordan valley, the attested community to southern Mesopotamia.

No spread route is recorded for Mandaeism.
