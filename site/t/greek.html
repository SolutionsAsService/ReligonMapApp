Ancient Greek religion

Zeus and the Olympians, the mysteries of Eleusis and the oracle of Delphi.

Dates
c. 1400 BCE – 529 CE (c. 1,928 years)
Origin
Mycenaean Greece (region)

About the date: Several Olympian gods are named in Mycenaean Linear B tablets (c. 1400 BCE). Public sacrifice was banned in 391–392 CE; the Athens Academy closed in 529.

How it ended (529 CE): Christian emperors banned the cults (391–392); philosophy schools closed in 529.
