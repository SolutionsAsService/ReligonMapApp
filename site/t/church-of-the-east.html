Church of the East

The church of the Persian empire, which carried Christianity along the Silk Road to China by 635. Today it continues as the Assyrian Church of the East.

Dates
c. 431 CE – present
Origin
Seleucia-Ctesiphon

About the date: Separates after the Council of Ephesus (431) and declares independence at Seleucia-Ctesiphon in 424–486.
