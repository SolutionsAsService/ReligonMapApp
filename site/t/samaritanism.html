Samaritanism

An Israelite religion with its own five-book Torah and its holy mountain at Gerizim. It survives today as a community of under a thousand people.

Dates
c. 400 BCE – present (c. 2,425 years)
Origin
Mount Gerizim

About the date: The Samaritan temple on Mount Gerizim was built around the 5th–4th c. BCE; Samaritans see themselves as the direct heirs of ancient Israel rather than a branch of Judaism.

No spread route is recorded for Samaritanism.
