Zoroastrianism

The faith of Zarathustra: the wise lord Ahura Mazda against the lie, good thoughts, good words, good deeds.

Dates
c. 1000 BCE – present (c. 3,025 years)
Origin
Eastern Iran / Central Asia (region)

About the date: Zarathustra's date is unknown; estimates run from about 1500 to 500 BCE. 1000 BCE is a midpoint, not a fact.
