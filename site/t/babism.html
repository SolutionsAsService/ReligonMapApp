Bábism

A messianic movement in Qajar Iran whose prophet, the Báb, was executed in 1850.

Dates
c. 1844 – c. 1866 (c. 22 years)
Origin
Shiraz
Branches
Bahá'í Faith

About the date: The Báb declared his mission in Shiraz in 1844; after 1866 most Bábís followed Bahá'u'lláh. A small Azali remnant lasted longer.

How it ended (1866): Most followers became Bahá'ís in the 1860s.

No spread route is recorded for Bábism.
