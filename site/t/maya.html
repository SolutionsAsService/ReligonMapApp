Maya religion

The calendar priests, kings and gods of the Maya, recorded in glyphs and the Popol Vuh.

Dates
c. 1000 BCE – 1697 (c. 2,696 years)
Origin
Petén lowlands (region)

About the date: Ceremonial centres from about 1000 BCE. Nojpetén, the last independent Maya kingdom, fell in 1697; Maya ritual continues today blended with Catholicism.

How it ended (1697): Spanish conquest; the last kingdom fell in 1697.
