Celtic religion

Dates
c. 600 BCE – 600 CE
Origin
Central Europe (Hallstatt) (region)

About the date: Hallstatt and La Tène cultures from about 600 BCE; Ireland is largely Christian by about 600 CE.

How it ended (600 CE): Roman conquest and Christianisation.
